# ---- College overview ----

what kind of college is TKM:TKM College of Engineering is the oldest engineering college in the private sector in Kerala. It was established in 1958 by Janab Thangal Kunju Musaliar under the TKM College Trust, and today it is an autonomous college affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University.

how many students study at TKM:The college admits more than 1000 students a year across nine undergraduate programmes, and about 250 students a year across its postgraduate programmes. That is the highest annual intake among peer colleges in Kerala.

how many teachers does the college have:The college has more than 200 faculty members, along with about 150 technical and ministerial staff.

how did the college begin:The college started with 120 students in three basic streams of engineering, and today it has the highest annual student intake among its peer colleges in Kerala.

who laid the foundation stone of the college:Dr. Rajendra Prasad, the first President of India, laid the foundation stone of the college on February 3, 1956.

who inaugurated the college:The college was formally inaugurated on July 3, 1958 by Prof. Humayun Kabir, who was then the Union Minister for Scientific and Cultural Affairs.

what is the vision of the college:The vision of TKM College of Engineering is excellence in education and research with a socio-economic and environmental outlook.

what is the mission of the college:The college's mission is to offer state-of-the-art undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes, to do cutting-edge research and collaborative projects with industry, to instill ethical, social and environmental perspectives for sustainable development, and to nurture creativity, self-learning and interpersonal skills.

when did the college get autonomy:The UGC conferred autonomous status on the college on 26 May 2022, and APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University formally notified it on 2 September 2022.

which university is the college affiliated to:The college has been affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University since 2015. Before that it was affiliated to the University of Kerala.

is the college approved by AICTE:Yes. The college and all its programmes are approved by the All India Council for Technical Education, AICTE.

which programmes have NBA accreditation:All the B.Tech programmes and two M.Tech programmes are accredited by the National Board of Accreditation. B.Tech Civil and Mechanical Engineering have six-year accreditation, which no other engineering college in Kerala has, and the other undergraduate programmes have three-year accreditation.

what is special about the college's NAAC grade:TKM is the only engineering college in the government and aided sector in Kerala to be accredited with an A grade by NAAC.

what is the NIRF ranking of the college:In the National Institutional Ranking Framework, the college was placed in the 201 to 250 band in 2019.

what recognitions has the college received:The college was rated top for value-for-money education in the India Today rating in 2021. Its NPTEL local chapter has a AAA grade, it is one of the few KTU colleges allowed to award B.Tech Honours, and it is one of four institutions in Kerala chosen for the National Doctoral Fellowship.

what is the Margadarshan scheme:AICTE selected TKM College of Engineering as a Margadarshan institute to mentor three neighbouring institutions, two engineering colleges and one polytechnic.

what is SEEDS 97:SEEDS-97, the Startup Enabled Entrepreneurship Development Centre, is a technology business incubation centre set up at the college by the 1997 alumni batch.

what is TEQIP:TEQIP is the World Bank assisted Technical Education Quality Improvement Programme. The college received 12.88 crore rupees under TEQIP-II between 2012 and 2023.

what is RUSA funding:TKM College of Engineering is the only engineering college that became eligible for funding under RUSA.

which institutions does the TKM trust run:The TKM College Trust runs TKM College of Arts and Science, TKM Institute of Technology, TKM Institute of Management, TKM School of Communication and Information Technology, TKM Centenary Public School, TKM High School, TKM Higher Secondary School, the TKM Centre for Advanced Learning and the TKM School of Architecture.

# ---- Founder ----

who was Thangal Kunju Musaliar:Janab A. Thangal Kunju Musaliar was a reformer, philanthropist and businessman, born on 12 January 1897. He formed the TKM educational trust in 1956, founded the college in 1958, and passed away on 19 February 1966.

why was the founder called the cashew king:Thangal Kunju Musaliar transformed Kerala's cashew industry in the 1940s and 50s and was known as the Cashew King. In 1940 the American magazine Fortune described him as the single employer who gave direct work to the largest number of people in the world.

what else did the founder do:He started the newspaper Prabhatham and the Janakshema Samajam, a people's welfare society, and wrote two books, Practical Advaita, the Law of Nature, and Communism, Capitalism and Socialism in the Present Context.

how is the founder remembered:In 1997, his birth centenary, the Postal Department released a stamp in his honour and a Philately Museum was set up at the college. In 2019 Kollam Corporation set up the A. Thangal Kunju Musaliar Park at Asramam.

# ---- Admissions ----

how do I get admission for btech:Eighty-five percent of B.Tech seats are government quota, filled from the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations rank list, which combines KEAM and higher secondary marks equally. The other fifteen percent are management quota seats, filled by the TKM College Trust from candidates declared eligible by the Commissioner.

what is the eligibility for btech:You need to pass plus two with physics and mathematics as compulsory subjects and chemistry as an optional subject, with at least 45 percent marks in these subjects together. For SC and ST candidates, a pass in the higher secondary exam is enough.

how do I get admission for barch:B.Arch admission is through the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations rank list, based on NATA and higher secondary marks. You need 50 percent aggregate in plus two with mathematics, or an equivalent diploma, and at least 40 percent in NATA.

how do I get admission for mtech:Seats in government aided M.Tech programmes are allotted centrally by the Directorate of Technical Education, and you need a B.Tech in the relevant branch. Candidates with a valid GATE score are eligible for a scholarship. In self-financing M.Tech programmes, half the seats are on merit and half are management quota.

how do I get admission for MCA:MCA admission is through the centralised allotment of the LBS Centre for Science and Technology, Thiruvananthapuram. You need a three-year degree with at least 50 percent marks, with mathematics, statistics, computer science, computer applications or engineering as a main or subsidiary subject. The minimum is 45 percent for SEBC and physically challenged candidates.

what is the management quota:Fifteen percent of the seats are management quota, filled by the TKM College Trust from applicants declared eligible by the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations.

are there seats for students from abroad:Yes. Up to fifteen percent extra seats, over the sanctioned intake, are for children of Indian workers in Gulf countries, people of Indian origin, and foreign students. There is also a Government of India quota for students from places like Andaman and Nicobar and Lakshadweep.

what is the fee for btech:Fees follow the government and university orders, and the college admits students from the Kerala government rank list at a nominal fee. I don't have the exact amounts, so please check with the admission office on 0 4 7 4, 2 7 1 2 0 2 2.

what is the btech lateral entry admission process:For B.Tech lateral entry, report at the admission help desk at the APJ Hall entrance to get a token, finish the e-TLAB portal registration, get your certificates verified, pay the admission fee by debit card or UPI at the PTA Hall, and collect your admission slip. Hostel admission has a separate help desk.

does the college keep original certificates:The college keeps students' documents only until the university completes its verification, and does not retain them after that.

how do I apply for the hostel:Hostel application forms are in the downloads section of the college website, and new students can complete hostel admission at the hostel help desk during admission.

tell me about the electrical and electronics department:The Electrical and Electronics Engineering department started in 1958 with 40 students. what research does the electronics department do:Electronics and Communication research covers wireless and digital communication, biomedical instrumentation and medical robotics, signal and image processing with machine learning, and chip design, semiconductor device modelling and AI hardware.

tell me about the chemical engineering department:The Chemical Engineering department offers a B.Tech with 60 seats. # ---- Rules ----

is smoking allowed on campus:No. The TKM campus is a smoking-free campus, and alcohol, tobacco and drugs are strictly prohibited on campus and in the hostels.

can I use my mobile phone in class:Students should avoid using mobile phones in classrooms, the library, the computer centre and exam halls, and use them only where permitted.

are political activities allowed on campus:No. As per a Kerala High Court order, politically based organisations are not allowed on campus, and students may not take part in strikes, processions or agitations.

can outsiders enter the campus:Outsiders cannot enter the campus or hostels without permission, though students may bring their parents and family members.

can I ride a two wheeler inside the campus:No. Two-wheeler riding is not allowed inside the campus. Students must park in the space reserved for them.

is there a dress code:Students are expected to dress decently, without obscenity.

what happens if a student breaks the rules:Minor violations lead to a warning or an apology, and serious ones can mean being barred from exams, suspension, paying for damage, losing the caution deposit, or expulsion. The Principal is the final authority on discipline.

what are the college rules:The student code of conduct asks students to respect authorities, staff, women and elders, dress decently, stay away from ragging, alcohol and drugs, avoid political activities and strikes, protect college property, and spend their free time in the library or reading room.

can students collect money or put up banners:Students need written permission from the college to collect money, and prior permission to put up banners, flags or boards.
